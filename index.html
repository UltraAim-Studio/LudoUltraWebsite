FAQ

How do I play with friends?

Create a private room, share the room code, and friends jump in instantly. You can also challenge specific friends from your contact list.

Is internet required?

Online multiplayer needs a connection. Pass & Play works fully offline — perfect for flights and family game night.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes — AI opponents with adjustable difficulty, so you can hone your routes at your own pace.

How do I customize my characters?

Head to the token screen in-game. Unlock new characters, items, and styles through gameplay, achievements, and the shop.

Is the game free?

Completely free to play. Optional in-game purchases are cosmetics and battle-pass items only.

Where can I report issues?

Use the in-game help menu or email support@ludoultra3d.com.
